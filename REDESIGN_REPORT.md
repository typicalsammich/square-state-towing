Replaced the dark industrial presentation with a warm ivory and forest-green identity, calmer typography, more whitespace, an editorial split hero, numbered service rows, clearer process steps, and coordinated company, service, area, gallery, contact, FAQ, and privacy pages.

Desktop fine-pointer devices use small green/copper cursor assets. Text fields keep a text cursor. Scrollbars use a coordinated green thumb and ivory track where supported. All 22 pages retain the verified company information and authorized photography. The contact form still prepares a request locally without claiming delivery. No new service, arrival time, review, equipment capability, or city coverage was invented.

Validation: static build and all page/asset/metadata checks pass. Mobile menu, live area selection, gallery modal, and contact preparation are checked in the browser. The narrow 309-pixel viewport has no horizontal overflow. The Vercel configuration explicitly declares `dist` as the output directory.
